# To video

Assemble reviewed scenes according to `STORYBOARD.md` and deliver through the selected HyperFrames workflow.

## Process

1. Recompute digests and verify stable beat/narration IDs and lineage: article → beats → narration → scenes → storyboard, with storyboard also matching narration. For content-driven work, require current content approvals through `ARTICLE.md` and `TOPIC.md` when applicable. Return mismatches to the owning stage.
2. Read [the shared project contract](../boring-video/references/hyperframes-review-project.md). Require its scene review gate and matching project identity/revision in scenes, storyboard, and project state. Load `/hyperframes` and its current route contract; continue the recorded route with the planning artifacts, source media, design constraints, and `BRIEF.md` choices.
3. Mount approved compositions, implement planned boundary transitions, and master whole-film audio under the shared contract's production continuation. Update canonical frame statuses in the same `STORYBOARD.md` as HyperFrames checks permit; preserve stable scene, frame, and narration IDs.
4. Verify source identity, scene playback parity, and joins under that contract. For affected screen sequences, also complete [screen-evidence viewing review](../to-scenes/references/screen-evidence.md); preserve `/record-terminal` replay evidence where used. Record actual reviewed intervals, limitations, and the validated revision. Route scene-internal corrections through `/to-scenes` before reassembly.
5. Complete the selected HyperFrames workflow's checks and assembled playback review. Export when requested, retaining revision identity on the delivered file. Keep the HTML scene review entry point available for targeted revisions.
6. When `BRIEF.md` requests publishing outputs, derive them from stable content, narration, design truth, and scene evidence. Record source digests and output paths in `PUBLISHING.md`; platform adaptation preserves claims and evidence status.

Complete when the requested assembly or revision passes the shared contract's parity/join checks and HyperFrames' workflow checks, with playback review and limitations recorded against the current revision. Requested exports must pass rendering checks; requested publishing outputs must have source-linked records.
