# To video

Assemble finished, reviewed HyperFrames scenes into the final video. Mount the approved scene HTML, add boundary transitions, master whole-film audio, and verify/export the assembled result under the current Markdown storyboard.

## Process

1. Confirm `BEATS.md`, `NARRATION.md`, `SCENES.md`, and `STORYBOARD.md` agree on stable beat and narration IDs. Recompute every artifact digest and confirm each immediate child records the current parent digest: article → beats → narration → scenes → storyboard, with the storyboard also matching its narration source. For content-driven work, also confirm approval digests match and lineage reaches an approved `content/ARTICLE.md` and an approved `content/TOPIC.md` when that contract exists. Return a conflict, digest mismatch, or stale approval to the skill that owns that artifact.
2. Open the exact project and reviewed revision recorded by `SCENES.md`, `STORYBOARD.md`, and project state; fail back to the owning stage if they name different projects or stale revisions. Require current passed scene review decisions and the corresponding composition paths in project state; return missing or stale review to `/to-scenes`. Load `/hyperframes`, let it select the workflow, then read that workflow's current route contract. Carry presentation as an input; do not infer a workflow from `faceless` alone.
3. Give the selected workflow the four planning artifacts, applicable content artifacts, source material, design constraints, presentation choice, collaboration preference, and voice choice. Continue implementation in that project instead of re-creating the reviewed visual direction. Treat the current `STORYBOARD.md` as the canonical HyperFrames plan: mount the finished reviewed scene compositions for its `outline` assembly frames and implement the planned boundary transitions, update that same Markdown as canonical statuses or explicit revisions change, and preserve stable frame and narration IDs. A material content revision returns to the owning content artifact and its approval gate before it propagates forward.
   - For terminal-hosted coding, shell, CLI, database, or TUI shots, **REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** Use `record-terminal` and carry its evidence-backed, seek-safe replay, selected visual treatment, and verification contract into production.
   - For screen evidence or explanatory visual sequences, read and apply [`../to-scenes/references/screen-evidence.md`](../to-scenes/references/screen-evidence.md) to the built timeline. Verify actual states and dependencies against the storyboard's selected temporal structure, including source entry and exit, readable holds, cue alignment, and framing purpose. Use its loaded-contract and acceptance-check requirements when resuming revisions as well as initial production.
   - Read [the assembly and scene parity contract](../boring-video/references/hyperframes-review-project.md#production-continuation). Verify approved source/asset digests and scene-local playback parity at the recorded timestamps, accounting for placement and declared transition overlaps. Return any scene-internal correction to `/to-scenes`, then reassemble its approved revision.
4. Reuse the delivery narration, subtitles, and scene-local effects already reviewed during `/to-scenes`, including ListenHub or MiniMax outputs when selected. Whole-film mastering may set global levels and cross-scene music continuity. Missing required delivery audio or a requested voice/text/timing change returns to `/to-scenes` and its voice review before assembly continues.
5. For each changed screen or explanatory visual sequence, complete the viewing review defined by the reference above. Record actual reviewed intervals and pending limitations in the existing project review record; renderer checks and completed hero stills cover different risks from this viewing review. Carry the latest validated project revision into any export, keeping prior exports identifiable as earlier versions.
6. Leave production inside the selected HyperFrames workflow through its own completion criterion. After its checks pass, use HyperFrames Studio for playable timeline, motion, transition, and final-look review; retain the HTML scene review entry point for targeted joint review. Verify joins and transition overlaps without changing approved scene-local content, animation, or timing. Scene-internal problems found here return to `/to-scenes` for correction and reapproval.
7. When `BRIEF.md` requests publication materials, continue the publication branch only after its source content and visual direction are stable. Use the approved article, narration, design truth, and scene evidence; record source digests and output paths in `PUBLISHING.md`. Platform adaptation may change length and framing, but not claims, evidence status, or visual identity.

The handoff is complete when HyperFrames has opened the reviewed project revision, accepted the planning artifacts and any requested external TTS outputs into its state, and owns the playable review through Studio. When requested, the source-linked publication branch is also recorded in `PUBLISHING.md`. For a production revision, completion also requires the affected viewing review and a recorded current project revision; when export was requested, deliver that revision through HyperFrames' rendering checks. Production starts from the approved scene evidence and storyboard plan; it does not invent a second planning artifact.
