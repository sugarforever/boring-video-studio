Principles

The rule & the failure

Do

  • Keep the ground warm. Parchment or warm-dark; every neutral yellow-brown-toned. Never pure white or cool grey.
  • Serif display, sans body. Newsreader 500, sentence case; Inter body at a relaxed 1.5–1.6.
  • Hold 40–55% open. Editorial whitespace is an active counter-weight — let it breathe.
  • Build contrast from size. One serif element at book-title scale; single weight throughout.
  • Use the one terracotta warmly. A soft underline, a small block, or a single flagged word.
  • Soften everything. Rounded cards (16–32px), whisper shadows, quiet sage marks.

Don't

  • Use cool blue-grey. No pure white, no cold neutral — the whole palette is warm.
  • Set headlines in sans, caps, or bold. Weight-500 sentence-case serif is the ceiling.
  • Add a second saturated hue. Terracotta is the only accent; keep the sage quiet.
  • Use sharp corners or hard shadows. No gradient, no blur — softness is core.
  • Crowd the frame. Don't drop body line-height below 1.4; the space IS the personality.
  • Use cold, geometric tech ornament. Marks are organic and hand-drawn-feeling.
