Principles

The rule & the failure

Do

  • Sit on #07080a. Near-black blue-tinted ground — never pure black. Float content in the void.
  • Pair every shadow. Outer ring + inset top highlight + soft drop. Depth is real, never a flat rectangle.
  • Track body positive. +0.012em, weight 500; display at 600, near-zero tracking. Airy, not shouty.
  • Spend red sparingly. A kicker tick, one flagged word, an alert rail, or the diagonal stripe motif.
  • Contain with hairlines. Barely-visible white rgba 0.06–0.10; cool-gray #252829 for solid dividers.
  • Hover by opacity. Interactions shift opacity or elevation — macOS-native, never a color swap.

Don't

  • Use pure black. #000000 kills the blue tint that makes it Raycast, not a generic dark theme.
  • Flatten the depth. No single-layer shadows, no borderless flat rectangles.
  • Make red pervasive. Blue is for interactive/info; red is the brand punctuation, one accent per frame.
  • Track body negative. Raycast deliberately uses positive spacing for dark-mode readability.
  • Mix typefaces. No serif, script, or decorative face outside Inter / JetBrains Mono.
  • Add ornament. No second saturated hue, no colorful gradient backgrounds — the void is the stage.
