Principles

The rule & the failure

Do

  • Snap to the grid. Every edge on a 12-column field + 12px baseline. Never eyeballed.
  • Flush-left, ragged-right. Tighten display with negative tracking; body tracking near zero.
  • Hold 40–60% empty. Whitespace is an active counter-weight, not leftover.
  • One element dominates 4–8×. Hierarchy is size + weight, not a device.
  • Use the one red sparingly. A block, a tick, a chart leader, or a single flagged word.
  • Generate geometry by rule. Arc widths double; dots on the baseline lattice.

Don't

  • Center or justify text. No right-aligned body, no letter-spaced lowercase.
  • Add a second hue. No gradient, shadow, blur, or rounded corner — ever.
  • Mix typefaces. No serif, script, or decorative face outside the system.
  • Eyeball or rotate casually. A diagonal, if used, sits at a defined angle.
  • Crowd the frame. Timid, evenly-sized type with no dominant whitespace reads as broken.
  • Use emotive stock imagery. Photography is objective, high-contrast, grid-cropped.
