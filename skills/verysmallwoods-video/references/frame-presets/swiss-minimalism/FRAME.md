# Swiss Minimalism — Frame (video / frame layer)

## Overview

Swiss Minimalism at frame scale is the **International Typographic Style rebuilt for 1920×1080** — the
rational, objective poster language of Josef Müller-Brockmann, Armin Hofmann, and Emil Ruder. The
message is served, not decorated: clarity, order, and a visible structural logic. Every element is
placed on a **strict 12-column grid over a 12px baseline** — nothing is positioned by eye. Hierarchy
comes from **weight, size, and position inside one grotesque family**, and drama comes from **extreme
scale contrast** (one giant word or number against tiny precise type) held in **40–60% negative
space**.

The palette is absolute restraint: a stark neutral **paper**, near-black **ink**, and **one saturated
signal red** used sparingly to flag the single most important thing. Flat fills only — the red is a
_block_, never a gradient or glow. The voice is a single superfamily: **Inter Tight** (heavy, negative
tracking) carries every display line flush-left; **Inter** carries body flush-left ragged-right; **JetBrains
Mono** carries the systematic chrome — section codes, ticks, data figures. Atmosphere, when any, is
**generated geometry**: concentric arcs whose widths double, dot matrices on the baseline lattice — never
freehand ornament.

**Key characteristics at frame scale:**

- **Strict 12-column grid + 12px baseline** — every block, rule, and image edge snaps; placement is never eyeballed.
- **Two colors + one red** — stark paper, near-black ink, ONE signal red; flat fills, zero gradient/shadow.
- **One grotesque superfamily** — Inter Tight display / Inter body / JetBrains Mono chrome; hierarchy from weight+size.
- **Flush-left, ragged-right — always.** Never centered body, never justified. Even word spacing.
- **Aggressive scale contrast** — one dominant display element at 4–8× its neighbor; the ratio IS the drama.
- **Asymmetry over symmetry** — content driven to a corner/edge on the grid, large empty counter-space.
- **Systematic geometry** — concentric arcs (widths double), dot matrices; generated by rule, one cluster per frame.
- **Right angles only** — `border-radius: 0` everywhere; circles/arcs are the sole curved forms.

## The Frame

### Frame Craft Bar

Five eyeball tests gate every frame before any structural check:

- **Squint** — exactly one Inter Tight element dominates at **4–8× its nearest neighbor** (`display`/`display-hero`/`vbig-numeral`); hierarchy is size+weight, never a decorative device.
- **Silence** — declarative frames read **40–60% empty**; whitespace is confident and large, an active compositional element — not sparse-by-accident and never crowded.
- **Grid** — every edge aligns to a **12-column field** and a **12px baseline multiple**; two elements that don't share a grid line read as broken. Flush-left, ragged-right.
- **Restraint** — **paper + ink + ONE red**; the red flags a single element; **zero** shadow / gradient / blur / rounded corner; at most one geometry cluster per frame.
- **Reference** — aim at a **Müller-Brockmann _Beethoven_ / _Musica Viva_ poster, a Hofmann _Giselle_, an Emil Ruder type plate**; failure looks like a **centered, rounded-card, shadowed SaaS deck**.

- **Primary:** 1920×1080 (16:9). Display authored in **`cqw`** (`px ÷ 1920 × 100 = cqw`).
- **Vertical:** 1080×1920 (9:16). **Square:** 1080×1080 (1:1).
- **Safe area:** `edge` (5cqw) generous gutters; the 12-column grid hangs from the `edge` line, geometry may bleed off an edge.

**The container law (load-bearing).** Every frame ground sets `container-type: size`; ALL frame-relative
units are `cqw`/`cqh` against it — never `vw`. The 12-column grid, the 12px baseline, the 1px hairlines,
and the arc widths all hold their proportion against the frame at any render size.

## Colors

Tokens are the identity. `{colors.paper}` is the stark neutral ground; `{colors.ink}` near-black is
headlines, body-in-ink, and every 1px rule; `{colors.ink-soft}` grey is secondary/body copy;
`{colors.accent}` signal red is the ONE accent — a filled block, kicker tick, chart leader, or a single
flagged word, used sparingly. `{colors.panel}` is the only secondary surface (image fields, module
fills). `{colors.grid}` (7%) is the optional construction-grid overlay, revealed as chrome, not
permanent.

**No second hue, ever.** When emphasis is needed: grow the type, add weight, move it on the grid, or
add ONE red element. Red never becomes a palette — it is a signal. Headlines are ink or red only;
body is ink or grey; the ground is paper.

## Typography

Three faces, one superfamily voice. The **display ramp** (Inter Tight `h3` 1.9cqw → `display-hero`
9.6cqw, weights 700–900, negative tracking) carries every headline flush-left. The **reading ramp**
(Inter body 0.85cqw grey / lede in ink, labels in px) carries copy + labels. The **chrome/data ramp**
(JetBrains Mono) carries section codes, ticks, and data figures.

- **Legibility floor:** any load-bearing line ≥ **1.4cqw**; px labels/mono are chrome only.
- **Fit-to-measure:** size the headline to its length. Cap the block at **≤ 62cqw** (headlines hang from
  the left margin, ragged-right). ≤3 words → `display`/`display-hero`; 4–6 → `headline`/`h2`; 7+ → `h3`.
  Hero stats use `vbig-numeral`/`stat-figure`.
- **Flush-left, ragged-right — always.** Never justify (rivers), never center body. Word spacing stays
  even. **Tighten** display with negative tracking; keep body tracking near zero; letter-space only short
  uppercase labels (`label`/`kicker` 0.12–0.14em) and mono.
- **Case:** headlines sentence case or lowercase; ALL-CAPS reserved for short `label`/`kicker`/mono chrome.
  Bold vs Regular weight contrast between display and body is a signature — make it dramatic.

## Depth & Surface

The flat plane is the only technique — there is no depth. Hierarchy comes from:

- **Scale contrast** — the 9.6cqw → 0.75cqw span; one giant element against tiny precise type.
- **Weight** — Inter Tight 800/900 display vs Inter 400 body.
- **Grid position** — asymmetric placement, corner weight, alignment to shared column/baseline lines.
- **The one red** — a single flat red block or word draws the eye.
- **Negative space** — 40–60% empty; emptiness carries as much as ink.

**Ceiling:** no box-shadow, no gradient, no blur, no elevated card, no rounded corner, no second hue.
The red is always a flat fill. Depth is structural and flat, never cast.

## Shapes

- **0 (right angle)** — every block, panel, bar, rule, image field. The default and the law.
- **50% (circle / arc)** — concentric-arcs and dot-matrix dots only; the sole curved forms, always systematic.
- Soft-rounded rectangles **do not exist**.

## Components

- **column-grid** — the invisible 12-column + 12px-baseline law everything snaps to (optional faint overlay as chrome).
- **hairline** — the universal 1px ink rule (occasionally red). **red-bar** / **kicker-tick** — flat red blocks for weight and the section marker.
- **concentric-arcs** / **dot-matrix** — the two systematic geometry signatures (red+ink, widths double / baseline lattice), one cluster per frame.
- **index-row** — numbered flush-left row on a hairline. **h-bar** / **kpi-bar** — flat data bars where width/height IS the value.
- **image-field** — the hard-cropped objective-photo slot (crossed-X until wired). **page-chrome** — mono section code / date / topic / № on the safe line.

## Frame Treatments

> Recipe: ground · grid anchor · composes · focal · chrome · accent · silence · Fixed/Free · density.
> Every frame is flush-left on the 12-column grid, 40–60% empty, one red accent, one geometry cluster max.

### 1 · Cover (identity · move: corner-weighted display + arc · lower-left)

**Ground** `{colors.paper}`, grid hangs from `edge`. **Composes** page-chrome (S01 / date), concentric-arcs
(upper-right, bleeding off), kicker-tick + kicker, display-hero. **Focal** a 2–3 line Inter Tight
`display-hero` headline in ink, driven to the **lower-left** and hung from the left margin (ragged-right),
with a red kicker-tick + `kicker` above and a short Inter lede below. **Chrome** mono section code /
date top; topic / № bottom. **Accent** the red arcs + the kicker tick (optionally one red word in the
headline). **Silence** ~55% empty — the counter-space upper-left is intentional. **Fixed** flush-left,
12-col grid, one red, arcs generated by rule. **Free** headline, arc scale/placement, lede. **Density** sparse.

### 2 · Index / Agenda (index · move: numbered hairline rows · left)

**Ground** `{colors.paper}`, `edge`. **Composes** kicker-tick + kicker, h2, 4–6 index-rows spanning
columns 1–9. **Focal** an Inter Tight `h2` over numbered index-rows (mono ordinal + Inter Tight label,
each on a 1px ink rule), one row optionally accent-red. **Chrome** page-chrome. **Accent** one red row
or red ordinals. **Silence** columns 10–12 held empty as the counter-margin. **Fixed** 1px ink rules,
flush-left, mono ordinals. **Free** items, count, which row is red. **Density** standard (generously spaced rows).

### 3 · Statement / Pull Quote (statement · move: giant flush-left word · left)

**Ground** `{colors.paper}`, `edge`. **Composes** kicker, display/display-hero, attribution, optional
dot-matrix (corner). **Focal** a 1–3 line Inter Tight statement flush-left hung from the margin, **one
key word set in red**; a small mono/`label` attribution beneath a hairline. **Chrome** page-chrome.
**Accent** the single red word (or a red-bar under the line). **Silence** ~55–60% — deliberately open
to the right. **Fixed** flush-left, negative tracking, one red word. **Free** statement, which word is
red, attribution. **Density** sparse.

### 4 · Data / Stats (data · move: flat bars on a baseline · left)

**Ground** `{colors.paper}`, `edge`. **Composes** kicker + h3, kpi-bar row (3–4 columns) or h-bar
stack, mono ticks. **Focal** either a KPI tower (vertical red bars, `stat-figure` figure below each,
`label` under a hairline) or a horizontal h-bar ranking (bar width = value, end-labelled); all baselines
on one 1px ink rule. **Chrome** page-chrome + a mono SOURCE line. **Accent** red bars (or one red leader
bar among ink bars). **Silence** moderate — the chart occupies the lower field, headline the upper-left.
**Fixed** flat square-ended bars, width/height = real data, hairline baseline. **Free** figures, bar
count, orientation. **Density** standard.

### 5 · Two-Column Editorial (content · move: asymmetric image + text · split)

**Ground** `{colors.paper}`, `edge`, 12-col split (e.g. text cols 1–5, image-field cols 7–12).
**Composes** kicker + h2 + Inter body in the text field; an image-field (crossed-X, hard-cropped to
its module) opposite. **Focal** an Inter Tight `h2` + flush-left Inter body, balanced against the
grid-locked image-field. **Chrome** page-chrome + a mono caption under the image. **Accent** a red
kicker-tick or one red word. **Silence** the gutter between fields; no filler. **Fixed** 1px ink field
borders, columns shared by both blocks, flush-left body. **Free** which side is text, split ratio, copy.
**Density** standard.

### 6 · Closing Manifesto (closer · move: red block + arcs · left)

**Ground** `{colors.paper}`, `edge`. **Composes** concentric-arcs or a full-height red-bar block (cols
1–2), display, a short mono sign-off, page-chrome. **Focal** a 1–2 line Inter Tight sign-off in ink
flush-left, anchored beside a full-height red-bar (or inside the largest arc cluster); a mono URL/`label`
beneath a hairline. **Chrome** page-chrome. **Accent** the red block / red arcs. **Silence** ~55%.
**Fixed** flush-left, one red element, generated geometry. **Free** sign-off, block vs arcs, meta. **Density** sparse.

## Composition Rules

### Do

- Snap **every** block, rule, and image edge to the **12-column grid** and **12px baseline** — placement is derived, never eyeballed.
- Set all type **flush-left, ragged-right**; tighten display with negative tracking; keep body tracking near zero.
- Drive the focal to a **corner/edge** and hold **40–60% negative space** as an active counter-weight.
- Make **one** Inter Tight element dominate at **4–8×** its neighbor; carry hierarchy on weight+size, not devices.
- Use the **one red** sparingly — a block, a kicker tick, a chart leader, or a single flagged word.
- Build atmosphere only from **generated geometry** (arcs whose widths double, dot matrices on the baseline), one cluster per frame.

### Don't

- Don't center or justify text; don't right-align body; don't letter-space lowercase.
- Don't add a second accent hue, a gradient, a shadow, a blur, or a rounded corner.
- Don't mix typefaces outside the Inter Tight / Inter / JetBrains Mono system; no serifs, no scripts, no decorative faces.
- Don't eyeball placement or rotate elements casually (a diagonal, if used, sits at a defined systematic angle).
- Don't crowd the frame or make type timid and evenly sized — no dominant whitespace and no hierarchy reads as broken.
- Don't use emotive/filtered stock imagery — photography is objective, high-contrast, hard-cropped to the grid.

## Aspect-Ratio Behavior

| Treatment              | 16:9                          | 9:16                                  | 1:1                          |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Cover                  | display lower-left, arc upper-right | display lower, arc top, 6-col grid | display lower-left, arc behind |
| Index / Agenda         | rows cols 1–9, margin right   | full-width rows, tighter leading      | rows cols 1–5 (of 6)         |
| Statement / Quote      | giant word flush-left         | word stacked, taller, red word kept   | word flush-left, centered block |
| Data / Stats           | KPI tower / h-bar horizontal  | h-bar vertical stack (drop KPI tower) | compact bars                 |
| Two-Column Editorial   | text + image side-by-side     | stacked (text over image)             | stacked                      |
| Closing Manifesto      | red block left, sign-off right| red block top, sign-off below         | red block behind, centered block |

Collapse the 12-column grid to **6 columns on 9:16** and **6 on 1:1**; keep the `edge` inset and the
12px baseline. Re-step display per ratio above the 1.4cqw floor. KPI towers lose meaning stacked on
9:16 — switch to a horizontal h-bar list. Flush-left holds in every ratio.

## Approved Entities

No real customers, logos, or vendors are defined here — render any such mark as a placeholder (the
crossed-X image-field, or a mono wordmark set in `label`). Photography slots stay as image-fields until
real objective imagery is wired.

## Numerals & Claims (hard rule)

Never invent figures, dates, or counts at frame scale. Render slots as `— figure —`, `{metric}`. **Bar
heights/widths must equal real data scaled proportionally** — a bar is a lie if its length doesn't trace
to a script number. Index ordinals (01, 02…) are decorative and may be sequential. The `SOURCE —` mono
line carries the citation when the script supplies one.

## Pre-Render Self-Audit

- **Squint** — exactly one Inter Tight element dominates at 4–8×; hierarchy is size+weight.
- **Silence** — declarative frames 40–60% empty; the counter-space is intentional, nothing crowded.
- **Grid** — every edge on a 12-column field + 12px baseline; flush-left, ragged-right; nothing eyeballed.
- **Palette** — paper + ink + ONE red only; red flags a single element; headlines ink/red, body ink/grey.
- **Type** — Inter Tight display (neg tracking) / Inter body / JetBrains Mono chrome; ≥1.4cqw floor; caps only on short labels.
- **Depth** — 0 shadow, 0 gradient, 0 blur, 0 rounded corner; bars/blocks flat and square-ended.
- **Geometry** — at most one arc/dot cluster, generated by rule (widths double / baseline lattice), red+ink.
- **Anchor** — corner/edge-weighted, asymmetric; no 3 frames in a row with the same anchor.
- **Fabrication** — every numeral and every bar length traces to the script, else a placeholder slot.

## Known Gaps

- **Motion intentionally out of scope.** frame.md specifies composition only; entrances/transitions are the animation layer's job (hard cuts and `expo.out`/`power4.out` snaps suit the style — nothing floats).
- **Fonts via Google Fonts.** Inter Tight + Inter + JetBrains Mono all ship on Google Fonts; they stand in for Helvetica Neue / Akzidenz-Grotesk / Univers (unavailable free). A CJK pairing (Noto Sans SC 400/700) carries the grotesque voice for Hanzi; keep flush-left and the weight contrast.
- **9:16 / 1:1 are guidance** — verify the 1.4cqw legibility floor, the grid collapse to 6 columns, and that KPI towers fall back to horizontal h-bars.
- **Concentric arcs, dot matrices, bars, and the crossed-X field are CSS/SVG-only** — no external imagery is required to render the identity.
